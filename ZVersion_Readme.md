# Promaia

## Dynamic Dual Memory System

Natural association. Auto-prioritizing. Blazing recall.

Postgres stores the facts. MuninnDB stores the *significance.* Connections strengthen through use. Irrelevant memories fade naturally. Ask a question — associative recall fires across a graph of connected ideas in under 20ms.

Every conversation auto-decomposes into **Decisions · Insights · Preferences · Asides** — each an independent node in the cognitive graph. You never organize anything.

---

## Fluid Natural Speech

Natural cadence, 2-way conversations.

Bidirectional audio — two simultaneous streams. Interrupt mid-sentence. She responds the instant you pause. Takes action while you talk: calendar events, memory lookups, email drafts — mid-conversation, no pause.

Full transcript capture after every call. Memories proposed, staged for review. Nothing permanent without your approval.

---

## Personal Agent

Knows you. Your world. Your priorities.

Ambient profiling from real interaction — cognitive style, energy patterns, communication preferences, neurodivergence awareness. Each trait tagged: **declared · inferred · confirmed.**

Shapes when she notifies, how she frames suggestions, how she speaks. Every installation becomes a different personality.
