Then and Now: The Writing Circle

Writing circle is thriving. It’s grown into a space full of magic.

Here’s the top level:

  • We’ve been meeting for 1.5 years.
  • Most sessions have had 5-6 attendees but September’s circle had 12!
  • We have all written at least 3 pieces per session: (12 + ~5) x 5 x 3 = 255
  • 255+ instances of processing emotion, bridging inner truths, and sharing laughter that this practice has brought into the world.

I’m so grateful for everyone who’s made it what it is today.

What’s Next: Dreamshare

The Writing Circle is magic, but every format has its limits. For the circle to hold more people, we would have to split into groups, which would change its chemistry. My intuition has been pointing toward a new, additional format for a long time.

Dreamshare is that format.

The first one is scheduled for December 11th 2025

A space for turning ideas into reality together…

Where we recognize that the future is spoken into existence.

The common thread is simple: we all dream. Maybe you're a seasoned expert ready to blow our minds, or maybe your story is just getting started. Either way, this is a place for you to cultivate and/or share your calling with an eclectic community of curious minds representing a microcosm of great SF Bay Area.

Lets explore together planting, nurturing and reaping the rewards of listening to our dreams–big or small!

Your dream can be anything…

  • Learning a recipe
  • Organizing a protest
  • Getting lost in the fog
  • Building a product
  • Making a film

You just have to be open to sharing the ride.

Builders, activists, and artists in one room? Experts next to people who don't know what they want yet? This is crazy! Damn right it is! But, I know first hand when it works, it's magic; a reality in which we may be seen from many perspectives.

Dreams unite us. Lets dedicate this time to revel in them, tune into our inner guides, and platform our intuition.

The Format: 50% Show and Tell + 50% unconference/mixer

We're going light on facilitation, heavy on serendipity; keeping enough structure that it's not cognitively taxing, but enough freedom that the unexpected can emerge.

  • Part 1: Show and Tell. A series of short, 5-minute lightning talks. Presenters share what they're building, learning, or dreaming about, the thoughts bubbling inside them. No polish required.
  • Part 2: The Mixer. An hour or so to do, share, eat free food, drink free drinks and be freely; Swap skills and find collaborators. This is where the bridge-building happens.

Our first gathering, Writing Circle taught us how to share our inner worlds. Dreamshare is where we'll build new ones together.
