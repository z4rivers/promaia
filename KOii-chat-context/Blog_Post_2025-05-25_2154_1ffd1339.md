## Dream Grow Share: A Crossroads of Perspectives

Hey guys, thank you all for trusting me and joining the Dream Grow Share (DGS) Discord!

I've been sculpting the vision behind DGS since August of 2024. In short, it's a cross-disciplinary accountability group built around the dream, grow, share loop. That means we're all out in the world dreaming, growing, and sharing.  Let's have a place to share our passions in a low-stakes, safe space, among an eclectic group of people.

Builders, ever wondered how an artist would perceive your ideas?
