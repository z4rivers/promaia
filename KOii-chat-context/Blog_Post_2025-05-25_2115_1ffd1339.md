## What is Dream Grow Share?

Dream Grow Share started as a simple idea: a "dream dojo." I envisioned a space where a hand-picked group of talented people with unique perspectives—techies, artists, scientists, writers, activists, and more—could share their journeys and gain perspective on their ideas. Think of it as the next evolution of my writing circle (which you can check out here: [/1dad1339696780689c6bc15d0e6f511a](/1dad1339696780689c6bc15d0e6f511a)).

It's a space for:

- **Accountability:** We keep each other on track.
- **Resource Sharing:** We share tools, knowledge, and connections.
- **Stepping Outside Our Domains:** We learn from each other's diverse perspectives.
- **Support:** We encourage each other to pursue our dreams and overcome obstacles.
Most importantly, it's about play. It's a huge privilege to play, and we need more platonic adult play in our lives. I'm deeply emotional, and when I channel those emotions, I change the world. Dream Grow Share is a place to do just that.

## Why is this important?

The systems established during the industrial age to prepare people for life are collapsing as the digital age rapidly emerges. We face existential threats but also incredible opportunities. I reject the notion that abstaining from capitalism is the solution. Capitalism is a tool, and it's up to us to learn how to use it effectively. The value lies in data and compute, and we have the ability to bypass traditional gatekeepers. We must learn to play the game.

We're on the verge of a total decoupling of labor and economic value. If eclectic communities like this one opt to sit this round out, there won't be another chance. We have love, trust, and global connectivity. We have rapidly advancing technology and unrestricted access to knowledge. We're going to win.
